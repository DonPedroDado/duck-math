# Phase 4.1: Russian - Context & Plan

**Written:** 2026-10-24 (without the GSD workflow). Inserted before Phase 5 Derivatives.
**Goal:** Russian (`/ru/…`) as a third language for the shell and all nine concepts, at the same quality bar as EN/DE.

## Decisions

- **R-01:** Locale `ru` beside `en` and `de`. English stays the base locale. The URL strategy is the same (`/ru/high-school/<concept>/<step>/`).
- **R-02:** **Russian school notation** in Russian formulas and stage labels: `tg`, `ctg`, `cosec` instead of `tan`, `cot`, `csc`. `sin`, `cos` and `sec` are unchanged. This is a small per-locale notation map, not translated text. EN and DE keep the international names.
- **R-03:** Claude writes all the Russian; the user reviews it in the app. Terminology follows Russian school usage: предел, асимптота, единичная окружность, область определения, непрерывность, разрыв.
- **R-04:** No words inside TeX. The one place that has some (`\text{vs. }` in Combine) moves the word out into a message, so KaTeX never renders Cyrillic (its fonts have none).

## Claude's discretion

- **Caption word budget for RU:** 20 (Russian has fewer words than English for the same idea, but longer ones).
- **Switcher:** three short codes, EN · DE · RU. Check the phone header still fits.
- Quotation marks «…», the decimal comma (via `Intl`), and the non-breaking-space thousands separator (via `Intl`).

## Slices

| # | Slice | Delivers |
|---|-------|----------|
| 1 | Locale plumbing | `ru` everywhere the locale list is fixed; notation map; RU template; tests |
| 2 | Shell + trig translations | `messages/ru.json` plus the 4 trig concepts |
| 3 | Functions + limits translations | the 5 remaining concepts |

### Slice 1: Locale plumbing
- `project.inlang/settings.json` locales, `src/concepts/locales.ts`, `CAPTION_MAX.ru` in `check-i18n.mjs`.
- `scripts/postbuild.mjs`: the root redirect allowlist, hreflang links, and the noscript link.
- `+layout.svelte`: switcher names and short codes (`shell_locale_ru_name` = "Русский", `RU`).
- `scripts/templates/concept/messages/ru.json.tmpl`; `new-concept` gets `--title-ru`.
- `src/lib/tex/notation.ts`: `trigTex(fn, locale)` (`\tan` or `\operatorname{tg}`) and `trigName(fn, locale)` for SVG labels. Used by the tangent formulas, Scene labels and readouts, and the identities special-angle line.
- Combine: "vs." becomes a message key outside the TeX.
- **Tests:**
  - Notation unit test.
  - i18n check fixtures with three locales.
  - e2e `/ru/` sets `lang="ru"` and uses a Russian title.
  - The switcher sets `dm-locale=ru`.
  - The root redirect honours a Russian browser.
  - The tangent page in RU shows `tg`.

### Slices 2–3: Translations
About 1,800 words. One commit per group, with `npm run check:i18n` green (parity, placeholders and word budget).

### Slice 4: Review pass
- Screenshot every concept's steps in RU, on desktop and phone (390 px).
- Look for overflow: Russian words are long, so check slider labels, chips and the step counter.
- Run the full suite, then you read through the Russian in the app.

## Risks
- **Long Russian words in narrow UI** (ParamSlider labels, guess chips, the scrubber tooltip). Slice 4 checks them on a phone.
- **Inter's Cyrillic subsets:** confirm the self-hosted font serves them (no fallback flash) in slice 1.
