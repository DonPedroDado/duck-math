<objective>
Ship formula-to-visual hover linking (MATH-03) once in the shared layer and prove it on the live Unit circle (D-04: only its cos/sin terms). Every later concept plan reuses `cls('t-x link-x')`, `linkAttrs()` and `linkOpacity()`.

Purpose: Research Pattern 7 and Pitfall 3. KaTeX DOM is replaced on every θ change, so naive per-node handlers get stuck or lost; delegation on the persistent wrapper plus pointermove resolution is the robust approach.
Output: link store + helpers, Tex delegation, linked unit-circle cos/sin, e2e link spec.
</objective>
