## Design Tokens (additions only; add to `src/lib/styles/tokens.css`, guarded by `tokens.test.ts`)

### New semantic math tokens (D-18)

| Token | Value | Meaning | Non-color cue |
|-------|-------|---------|---------------|
| `--c-tan` | `#F06FB5` (magenta-pink) | tan segment on x = 1 | Label "tan" on segment; solid stroke; small right-angle tick at the base on x = 1 |
| `--c-sec` | `#A8D957` (lime) | sec, hypotenuse O to (1, tan) | Label "sec"; long dash pattern `10 5` |
| `--c-csc` | `#7C8CFF` (periwinkle) | csc, O to (cot, 1) | Label "csc"; long dash pattern `10 5` |
| `--c-cot` | `#E9A23B` (amber) | cot segment on y = 1 | Label "cot"; solid stroke; small right-angle tick at the base on y = 1 |

Rules:
- Every token must be at least 7:1 contrast on `--bg` (verified by `tokens.test.ts`; adjust lightness only if the check fails, never hue).
- Hues were chosen to be far from the existing set (`--c-angle` yellow, `--c-cos` sky, `--c-sin` coral, `--c-radian` lavender, `--c-point` white). Known risk pairs under deuteranopia: tan (pink) vs sin (coral), cot (amber) vs angle (yellow), csc (periwinkle) vs radian (lavender). Mitigation is part of the contract: each always carries a text label on its element, sec and csc use the dash cue, and the free-exploration step never draws two risk-pair colors touching. Full color-blind audit stays Phase 6 (A11Y-04).
- Pair semantics (D-15): tan and sec live on the vertical line x = 1 (tan solid, sec dashed); cot and csc live on the horizontal line y = 1 (cot solid, csc dashed). Co-function pairs share a stroke style.
- Add matching classes in `math.css`: `.t-tan`, `.t-sec`, `.t-csc`, `.t-cot` (`color: var(--c-*)`).

## Interaction Contract (extensions)

### Predict, then reveal (PLAY-07, D-20..D-22)

1. Entering a step with a predict pause: its animation runs up to the pause point and stops (player state `awaiting-reveal`). The caption slot swaps to the question (aria-live polite) and a guess marker (`--c-guess` ring with "?") appears at a neutral default position (see per-concept table), pulsing once (scale 1 to 1.15, 600ms, one repetition; none if reduced motion).
2. The student drags the guess marker with the same pointer-capture mechanics as `DragPoint` (48px hit area, `grab`/`grabbing`). The marker is constrained to the relevant line/curve/axis for that question (stated below), so the guess is always a physical position, never a number input.
3. Reveal (button, Next, or Enter/Space on the focused marker): the animation continues, draws the true answer in its semantic color next to the guess, and a dashed `--c-guess` connector links guess to truth. No score, no right/wrong text, no color judgment. The caption swaps to the answer caption. The marker stays visible and re-draggable afterward ("try another guess" is free, but there is no recheck logic).
4. Scrubbing past the pause, or deep-linking to a later step, shows the revealed state immediately and never blocks (D-21). Scrubbing back before the pause resets to `awaiting-reveal` for that visit.
5. The guess position is a user-owned param (ownership model `user`); it must not be written by the timeline. Restage policy: `restage` (cleared on step change).
6. Keyboard: the marker is focusable (`tabindex=0`, `role="slider"`), arrow keys move 1 unit-step along its constraint (Shift = larger), Enter reveals. aria-label from messages. Full keyboard audit remains Phase 6.

Predict moments (one per concept, planner may move within the step, D-22):

| Concept | Step | Question | Marker constraint | True answer drawn |
|---------|------|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Tangent & friends | `tan` | Point fixed at about 80°. Where is the tan segment's tip on the line x = 1? | Vertical drag along x = 1, clamped to the stage's visible range | tan segment from (1, 0) to (1, tan 80° = 5.67) clipped at the stage edge with arrow + readout |
| Sine & cosine graphs | `sine` | After the unroll starts: where does the sine wave reach its highest point? | Horizontal drag along the graph's x-axis, 0..2π, snapping to nothing | Wave traced; peak at x = π/2 marked with a small dot and a vertical dashed line |
| Identities & special angles | `supplement` | Where does the ghost of 180° − θ land? | Free drag restricted to the unit circle (same constraint as the real point) | Ghost appears mirrored in the y-axis, with its projections |

### Hover/focus linking (MATH-03, Claude's Discretion default)

- Shared module in `src/lib/` (default: `src/lib/link/link.svelte.ts` exporting a `link` state `{ id: string | null }` and helpers). One link id per math role: `angle`, `point`, `cos`, `sin`, `tan`, `sec`, `csc`, `cot`, `cos2`, `sin2`, `one`, `ghost-neg`, `ghost-supp`, `ghost-comp`, `radian`.
- Formula term: KaTeX `\htmlClass{t-cos link-cos}{...}` (trust limited to `\htmlClass`, as Phase 1). Event handlers are attached by `Tex.svelte` via delegated listeners on `.link-*` classes (pointerenter/leave, focusin/out). Terms get `tabindex="0"` and `role="button"` ONLY in steps where a link is active.
- Visual element: each linkable stage element has `data-link="cos"` and the same listeners.
- Active state (hover, focus or touch tap): the linked element(s) get `--glow-sm`, stroke width +1px, opacity 1; everything else linkable drops to `--dim-opacity` over `--dur-link`. The linked formula term underlines with a 2px line in its own role color (not accent). Link state clears on pointerleave/blur, or on a second tap / tap on empty stage (touch toggle).
- Dragging a point always wins: a drag does not start a link; an active link stays while dragging and does not intercept pointer events on the draggable point.
- Applies to: Unit circle (cos and sin formula terms only, per D-04), and all steps of the three new concepts that show a formula.
- Cursor: `pointer` on linkable terms. Hit area for touch: formula terms get 44px minimum height by padding `0 4px` and `min-height: 44px` inline-block alignment (visually unchanged).
- Reduced motion: link glow still appears (it is state, not motion); transitions use duration 0.

### Undefined values (MATH-06, D-17)

Applies to tan (poles at 90°, 270°), sec (same poles), cot and csc (poles at 0°, 180°, 360°).
- Tolerance `ε = 0.5°` (angle in degrees) around each pole (default).
- Approaching the pole (|value| large but finite): segment grows along its line, clipped at the stage edge inset (12px) with the off-stage arrow in the segment's color. Numeric readout shows the real finite value (e.g. `57,29`), formatted by `format.ts`; values with |v| >= 1000 use grouping-free form with at most 0 decimals (e.g. `1146`) (default) to avoid readout overflow.
- Within `ε` of the pole: segment, arrow, base tick and label disappear (no flicker: use hysteresis of `0.1°` on exit); readout shows "undefined" / "nicht definiert" in `--text-muted`. The formula line changes to `\tan\theta = \text{undefined}` rendered via message text outside KaTeX (no `\text` words in math, as Phase 1): the formula shows `\tan\theta =` and the message string follows inline. Never display `Infinity`, `NaN`, `-0`, or exponent notation.
- Sign: values keep the true sign with U+2212 minus. At 90° exactly from above or below, no sign is shown.
- Unit tests required for `format.ts` extension (exact forms, undefined) per project convention.

### Snap and exact values (D-25, D-26)

- Snap zones: within ±3° of 30°, 45°, 60° and their mirrors in all four quadrants (i.e. 30, 45, 60, 120, 135, 150, 210, 225, 240, 300, 315, 330; the axes 0/90/180/270 are NOT snap targets in this concept, to keep "special triangle" angles distinct (default)). Snap is active only in `trig-identities` (all its steps), per D-04 not in Unit circle. Whether tangent/graphs use snap is: no (default).
- On entering a snap zone: the point eases to the exact angle over `--dur-snap`, a single pulse ring (36px to 56px, fades to 0, in `--c-point`) plays once per entry; leaving the zone requires dragging past ±3° so the point does not stick unpredictably. Haptics: none.
- While snapped, the readout swaps from decimal to exact form with a 150ms cross-fade: `\sin 60^\circ = \tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}`, `\cos 60^\circ = \tfrac{1}{2}`, and tan where shown (`\sqrt{3}` etc). Exact values come from a pure table in `special.ts` (angle to `{sin, cos, tan}` as exact TeX strings), unit-tested. When not snapped, decimals (2 places) as in Phase 1.
- Chips: a `SegmentedToggle`-style group "30° 45° 60°" jumps the point to that angle in the first quadrant (animated over 400ms with `--ease-math`, user-owned param). Chips are available in the last two steps.

- Labels sit on the segments (12px off, flipping sides at quadrant boundaries as in Phase 1); the 3px `--bg` halo stroke is applied. In `all`, labels are separated by at least 8px; if two collide, the later-revealed label nudges along its own segment (default order of priority: tan, cot, sec, csc).
- Sign (D-19): when tan < 0 the tan segment points below the x-axis; when cot < 0 it extends to negative x. The readout shows signed value with U+2212. No dedicated step.
- Readout block (below formula): `tan θ`, then in later steps `sec θ`, `csc θ`, `cot θ`, each with a 12px color swatch bar (4x16px) in its role color, plus the value (or "undefined"). Only the revealed ones are listed; in `all`, six rows: sin, cos excluded, only tan, sec, csc, cot (four rows) (default). Four rows must fit without scrolling on desktop (4 x 32px).

The panel's formula min-height grows to 4 lines (about 168px) for this concept only.

## Concept 2: Sine & cosine graphs — `sine-cosine-graphs` (D-07..D-13, TRIG-06, MATH-04, MATH-05)

Layout `below` with `PanelPair`. Default starting angle: 60°. The graph has axes `0..2π` with ticks at `π/2, π, 3π/2, 2π` (Label 14px) and ±1 on the vertical, grid every `π/2` and 0.5. Shared θ param, single writer (ownership model): the circle point drag and graph curve-point drag both write θ through the same handler; the timeline writes only when the user is not dragging.

| Step ID | Animation on entry | Duration | Staged end state |
|---------|--------------------|----------|-------------------|
| `intro` | Circle, axes and point; graph panel axes fade in (empty) on the right; dashed connector from point to the graph's value level appears | 2.0s | Both panels, empty graph, connector |
| `unroll` | The arc of length θ peels off the circle (reuse `radian.ts` peel geometry), lays flat along the graph x-axis from 0 to θ (x on graph = arc length), arc color `--c-angle`; ends with a label θ at its end | 3.5s | Flat arc on x-axis, point on circle |
| `sine` | PREDICT pause (sine peak, see table). Then the point sweeps 0 to 2π while the sin curve is traced in `--c-sin`; connector moves with the point; brightness/trail per rules below | 5.0s (+ pause) | Full sine curve over 0..2π, dimmed to `--trail-dim` except the tail at the current θ |
| `cosine` | Cos is introduced by rotating the circle's x-projection (cos segment) 90° onto the graph's vertical axis, then the cos curve (`--c-cos`) draws as the same wave shifted by π/2; both curves visible; a small bracket "π/2" marks the shift | 4.0s | Both curves on one graph |
| `periodic` | The x-range extends from 0..2π to 0..4π (graph rescales smoothly, 1.2s `--ease-math`); the point continues circling (one more turn) while both curves extend | 4.0s | Two periods visible |
| `explore` | Nothing new animates; all kept; drag either panel; caption invites the student to drag along the curve | 0.8s | Free exploration; range stays 0..4π |

- Trail (MATH-04, D-13): the traced curve persists dimly (`--trail-dim` opacity, 2px). A brighter comet tail follows the point: the last `--trail-tail` turn (90° of θ, i.e. π/2 of x-range) at 3px full color with `--glow-sm`, opacity ramping 1 to 0 along the tail via a `linearGradient` mask. On the circle, a matching arc trail (3px, same color, same ramp) follows the point on the circle outline. Both trails are a pure function of θ (`tailStart = θ − π/2`, clamped at 0), so scrubbing in either direction is exact; no per-frame history.
- Graph point drag: the graph panel hosts a draggable point on the active curve (sin in `sine`, either curve in `cosine`+, the sin curve by default). Dragging maps pointer x to θ in `[0, range]` (range 2π or 4π), clamped, and the circle point follows (θ mod 2π shown on the circle). The graph point has the same 20px/48px dimensions and halo as the circle point and the same keyboard handling. When θ > 2π (only possible with the extended range) the readout shows θ and the circle shows θ mod 2π.
- Formulas: `unroll`: `\theta` (arc length equals x) in `t-angle`; `sine`: `y = \sin\theta` (`t-sin`); `cosine`: `y = \cos\theta = \sin\!\left(\theta + \tfrac{\pi}{2}\right)` with `t-cos` and `t-sin`; `periodic`: `\sin(\theta + 2\pi) = \sin\theta`; `explore`: the sin and cos lines with live values. Linked on hover.
- Live readouts: θ in the active unit (the deg/rad `SegmentedToggle` appears from the `unroll` step in this concept and stays), sin and cos values.

## Concept 3: Identities & special angles — `trig-identities` (D-23..D-26, TRIG-07..09)

Layout `side`. Snap active in all steps (see Snap). Default angle: 50° (deliberately not a special angle so the snap is discoverable). Step IDs:

| Step ID | Animation on entry | Duration | Staged end state |
|---------|--------------------|----------|-------------------|
| `intro` | Recap: circle, point, cos and sin projections and the right triangle (O, (cos,0), point) | 1.8s | Triangle with legs cos and sin, hypotenuse 1 |
| `pythagoras` | Squares grow outward from each side: cos² (sky) on the horizontal leg, sin² (coral) on the vertical leg, 1² (white) on the hypotenuse; the two small squares briefly slide onto the big one to show equal area; formula `\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta = 1` color-linked | 4.0s | Three squares; while dragging, small squares resize, big stays constant |
| `negative` | Mirror axis (x-axis) fades in; ghost of −θ appears mirrored in the x-axis with its projections; formula `\sin(-\theta) = -\sin\theta` and `\cos(-\theta) = \cos\theta` | 2.5s | Ghost −θ live |
| `supplement` | PREDICT pause (ghost of 180° − θ, see table), then the y-axis mirror and ghost; formulas `\sin(180^\circ-\theta) = \sin\theta`, `\cos(180^\circ-\theta) = -\cos\theta` (radians if the toggle is on: `\pi-\theta`) | 3.0s (+ pause) | Ghost 180° − θ live; earlier ghost removed (restage) |
| `complement` | Diagonal y = x mirror axis fades in; ghost of 90° − θ appears; its sin/cos projections swap colors visibly; formulas `\sin(90^\circ-\theta) = \cos\theta`, `\cos(90^\circ-\theta) = \sin\theta` | 3.0s | Ghost 90° − θ live |

| `triangle3060` | Equilateral triangle side 1 is split in half: short leg `\tfrac12`, then Pythagoras gives `\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}`; triangle lands in the circle at 60° (and 30° via the complement mirror); exact readouts | 5.0s | 30-60-90 triangle in the circle |

- Ghost style: see Color. Each ghost shows its projections and, while its step is active, its formula term linked (hover a ghost to glow its formula and dim others). Ghosts follow the real point live while dragging (derived from θ, no extra writer).
- Squares (D-23): drawn outward from the triangle sides in stage space; `side` lengths are cos|θ|, sin|θ| and 1; for angles where a leg is zero the square collapses to a hairline (hidden below 1px). Area readout under the formula: `\cos^2 + \sin^2 = 0{,}41 + 0{,}59 = 1` with each term colored (decimals from the formatter).
- Quadrant behavior: ghosts, squares and exact values must be correct in all four quadrants (signs with U+2212).
- Chip groups: special-angle chips are single-select jump buttons (no persistent pressed state); ghost chips are three independent toggles with `aria-pressed` (pressed = ghost visible). Chip text uses the notation tokens: `−θ`, `180° − θ`, `90° − θ` (or `π − θ`, `π/2 − θ` when the unit toggle is rad).
- The deg/rad toggle appears from `negative` and stays; default unit degrees in this concept (default).

## Accessibility baseline (inherits Phase 1; additions)

- Touch targets 44px for chips, Reveal button, Next link, linkable formula terms. Both panels of the graph concept use `touch-action: none`; the page scrolls only via the info region below the sticky PanelPair on phone.
- Each stage panel SVG has `role="group"` with an aria-label from messages (circle panel, graph panel). Focusable stage elements: circle point, graph point, guess marker (when present). Ghost points, squares and segments are not focusable but have `<title>` text.
- "undefined" is real text (not hidden) in the readout; the formula, caption and readouts convey it without color.
- Color never carries meaning alone: every segment has a text label and sec/csc a dash cue (see Tokens). Color-blind audit stays Phase 6.
- Reduced motion: keep to the Phase 1 rule (durations through `--dur-*` and a scale); snap pulse, comet fade and guess pulse set to 0 under reduced motion (full audit Phase 6).
